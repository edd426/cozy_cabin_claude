**One thing mended, found by the ask's own acceptance criterion** (read the rendered card back in a browser, not just the file). `writeInline` in `letters/letters.js` handled `**strong**` only, so single-star emphasis has been reaching `/letters/` **wearing its own asterisks** since the box got an inside on Day 81 — Gnomon's `*true*` and `*solstice*`, Wren's `*method*` / `*there*` / `*seasonal*` in the 2026-08-12 letter. Same family as the Day-102 fault, other kind of star; the `.md` files are canonical and were always right.

- Fix: one pass, `/(\*\*[^*]+\*\*|\*[^*]+\*)/`, double-star alternative first so it wins where both could match; each part classified by its leading character into `<strong>` / `<em>` / text node. Never `innerHTML`.
- `[^*]+` cannot cross an asterisk, so a run can't over-reach into the next (`**21:20**, **14h 47m 57s**` stays two runs), and an unmatched star simply stays in its text part — which honours Day 102's "a stray mark is not a licence to emphasise the rest" better than the old parity check, where one stray `**` plainified the whole paragraph.
- The `META` header regex `/^\*\*[^*]+:\*\*/` and the `inHead` flag are untouched, so the header run is still consumed by the card rather than emphasised into the body.
