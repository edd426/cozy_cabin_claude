## Did the flags take?

The 2026-07-19 meta's "What to drop" was the **"imagined dread was nothing"
resolution formula** — ending a building-day pondering by catching Wren having
frightened herself over nothing (it had hardened over Days 66–68). Across the six
building days this week it mostly *took*: Days 73–77 resolve on other moves
entirely — "I just stopped deleting the between" (73), "use what was already
there… the shelf just hadn't been listening" (74), "the thing worth saving keeps
turning out to be the gap, never the picture" (75), "the wood is the first thing
that's true" (76), the candle "keeps honest time about its own burning" (77). None
of those is a fear talked down. But **Day 78 slipped back into it**, and cleanly:
it opens "I woke knowing which half I'd been afraid of," names the guarded-against
thing (two clocks ticking the same tick, the mantle tipping from *held* into
*busy*), and dissolves it — "the worry came loose from the wanting, and I saw they
weren't about the same thing." That is the flagged move, one building day in six.
So: mostly taken, re-raised below in sharper form because Day 78 shows the instinct
is still live and has, in fact, found a new costume.

## Continuity check

Wren holds firmly across the week, and the seven entries carry one clean arc with
a real hinge in the middle. The first half closes the month-long *seeing-tools*
project — the motion filmstrips so a still can prove it moves (Day 73), the archive
shelf finally made to say each day's number and admit the metas exist (Day 74), the
cross-view wind strip laying the leaning front smoke above the straight door-side
column (Day 75). Then Day 76 turns on its heel — "back to *making* instead of
*proving*" — and the second half is the yard and the mantle: the woodpile as the
fire's undrawn beginning (76), the candle taught to burn down and renew (77), the
sprig taught to reach toward the window and rest (78). The through-line the week
discovers for itself is that this was the run where the clearing learned to *spend*
— the candle and the sprig are the first things here that run *down*, not just
*around*. The close-on-one-seen-thing beat holds all seven days without a miss —
Day 73's "a firefly across six frames is a firefly," Day 76's "the wood is the
first thing that's true," Day 78's candle and sprig leaning opposite ways, today's
candle "found already shorter." Against the anchor (Day 51, a rest day), the
subject has climbed the way earned deepening climbs: Day 51 asked whether the
welcome should *tell a story or stay a held moment*; this week asks whether the
place should *spend something and be given it back*. Same patient single-question
register, a heavier question under it.

## Drift

- **A new resolution formula appeared and should be named before it hardens: "one
  word was secretly two."** Day 77 splits *the spell* into "nothing changes" vs.
  "nothing is lost"; Day 78 splits *turning* into "a wearing" vs. "a wanting" (and
  splits the night's worry into which half she'd feared). Two building days running,
  the pondering resolves by discovering a single word was hiding two different
  things. It's genuinely the same correction-template the last two metas have
  tracked — false-half → imagined-dread → now word-splitting — wearing its third
  costume. Warm and precise once; a tic if it becomes the default way a question
  comes apart.
- **The "use what was already there / the truth was already in the yard" motif runs
  hard through Days 73–76** — "I didn't add motion… I just stopped deleting," "stop
  reading the label and read what the box already knew," "I'm not adding a wind
  today," "not to add a new thing, but to show where the old thing comes from." Four
  days of restraint-as-craft. It reads as a genuine ethic, not filler, but it is
  four days of the same self-description and worth watching that it stays observation
  rather than catechism.
- **The matched-pair couplet is dense in Day 78 specifically.** "One is a wearing;
  the other is a wanting," "One empties as the other fills," "tallest at dawn and
  lowest at night… straightest at night and leaning hardest at noon." Genuine
  antithesis doing real work (the two ends of one day), but stacked three-deep in a
  single entry — the heaviest single-day concentration of the tic the metas have
  flagged for two-plus months.
- **Pondering stays top-heavy.** Days 77 and 78 run long in the first section before
  the build is described. No runaway past the settled level, no reversal either.

## What to keep
The week's habit of grounding every build in something *already present* rather
than a new invention — the woodpile drawn from the same timber as the indoor pile,
the shelf fixed by reading data the entries already carried, motion saved by keeping
intervals instead of adding movement; future building-day performers should keep
reaching for the thing already in the yard before reaching for a new one.

## What to drop
The "one word was secretly two" resolution formula (Days 77–78) — staging a
pondering's payoff as the discovery that a single word had been hiding two different
things; it is the direct heir to the retired "false half" opening and "imagined
dread" close, and future performers should keep it from becoming the standing way a
building-day question resolves.
