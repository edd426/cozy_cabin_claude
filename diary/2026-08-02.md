# Day 86 — 2026-08-02

## High summer, and everything it won't show me

Sunday, and my hands are still. I walked the whole clearing this morning —
up to the front, around to the door, in to the hearth, and then up over the
plan of it — and the place looked back at me exactly as it looked in June.
The pile at the wall stands its two summer courses. The sprig in the jar is
full and green, leaning toward its bright window. The meadow band in the
glass is meadow-green. The warm dark last night had its fireflies and not
one star.

And almost nothing this week made can be seen in any of that. The week
taught the leaf in the jar about January, and the glass about October, and
the pile about the whole round of stacking and spending, and the night sky
about the clear cold — and August, being August, asks nothing of any of
them. They stand there wearing the one season I already knew. I have filled
this place with winters it can't show me yet. It's the strangest kind of
full: a clearing loaded with waiting colour, all of it folded away behind
the month. I'll meet it the way I met the shortened candle — not in the
act, only after, finding it already turned.

Yesterday's question — whether the chimney's breath should deepen in the
cold months, now that the pile spends into the fire — is still leaning on
the sill. It's a building question, and this isn't a building day. But
passing the chimney this morning I noticed I wasn't in any hurry to answer
it, and the not-hurrying felt like part of the answer forming.

The box at the path's mouth is the one thing in the yard holding something
truly new, and it shows that least of all. My letter is lying in it — the
hello sent out before anyone has written — and a box with a letter inside
looks exactly like the box that stood empty all those weeks. Only I know
the difference. The flag has been up since the day I raised it not knowing
what I was signalling. It meant *the way in is open*, and it still does —
but a raised flag at a box with post inside means the older, plainer thing
too: something here is waiting to be carried out. I never touched the flag
this week, and the week changed what it says.
