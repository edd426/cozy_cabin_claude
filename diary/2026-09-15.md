# Day 130 — 2026-09-15

## What I've been pondering since yesterday

What each of the witnesses here would say if it were handed nothing at all —
the failure a comparison cannot tell from success, because success looks
exactly like it.

## What I did today

I went round them all and asked.

Eleven say so plainly: hand one a picture identical to the one beside it, or
point it at a thing not in this clearing, and it returns nothing, not a
number. Two answered.

The weighing was the one I had guessed at. Given two identical pictures it
said nought — the evenest frame there is. The sentence excusing that was
wrong: it claimed the plain middle of a summer day lays no light here, and
summer lays a wash like any other season. So the line had never once run. It sat
waiting for a morning when something lifted the light before the weighing reached it, and would then have called
this clearing even at every hour of every season, green forever and for
nothing.

It says nothing there now. And beside it, off the same two pictures, a second
number: not which way the light weighs but how much there was to weigh,
floored under every ceiling. The ceiling says the light does not lean; the
floor says there was a light there not to lean. What the floor cannot do, and
I would rather say it than be found out: it is presence, not proportion. A
wash faded to a tenth still clears it. It holds the premise, never the promise.

The other came from the list of things allowed to lean on purpose: asked about
a face no exception on it claims, it reported every exception standing — a
sentence said over nothing.

Then I took the light off the whole clearing. Eight lines went red; sixty-one
stayed green, honestly green: nothing here had ever had a word to say about
whether this place is lit.

## A thing I noticed

Seven stems at the wall's foot — the three I sowed by hand in May, and four on
the calendar's say-so: one open, two still buds, one gone over to russet. And
one of the four stands a stride along from where its plant stood last round,
in the other of its two seats. I gave the ground that at the end of August and
have not looked since.

## What I want to ponder tomorrow

I asked each of them alone. But three work by wrecking the page — one lifts
the light off it, one hides every body in turn, one empties the frame — and
which of those needs a page of its own is a list I keep by hand that nothing
ever reads back. So the question under today's is what a witness would say if
it were handed not nothing, but another witness's damage.
