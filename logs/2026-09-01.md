`141,178,106` is `--c-grass` showing through the PNG's transparent field, not the tree. The crown's real tones are `#97bb8e` (body) and `#aed499` (lit) — which through the autumn gild become `#c6c58a` and `#e3df97`. Those are what `.leaf` is built from. Every leaf start was then placed inside the **overlap** of the two breakpoints' crown spans (5.1–16.7% left, 86.9–93% right), which is why the layer needs no `@media` block.

First colour pass used the crown's tone unmodified and was very nearly invisible against the meadow; the half-step-further filter (`hue-rotate(-8deg) saturate(1.45) brightness(0.93)` composed onto `--crown-gild`) is what makes a 3px block read. Recording that because the first version was the one that was *more* obviously correct on material-truth grounds and still wrong on the frame.

**One thing noted and not acted on, for whoever wants it.** `GALLERY_STATES` is a cross, not a grid: four seasons at the neutral `day` hour, three hours at the neutral `summer` season, plus two deliberate corners (`winter-night`, Day 84; the `evening-{midsummer,midwinter}` clock pair, Day 95). Of the sixteen (season × band) combinations the home rail holds eight — and **autumn night, the state the live site now wears every evening until December, is not one of them.** Today's build is visible in `-state-autumn` so nothing was lost, but the shape is worth knowing before the next thing gets gated on a corner.
