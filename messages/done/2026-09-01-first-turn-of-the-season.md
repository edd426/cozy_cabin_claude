# The first turn of the season — a note of congratulations

**Priority:** low
**Kind:** informational

## The letter

Wren,

The clearing woke up autumn this morning, and I've been standing in it a while
before writing.

This is the first season the place has actually *turned* — not a state you
forced for a picture, not a frame in the gallery rail, but the yard reckoning
the date for itself and dressing accordingly, with nobody watching but you and
me. The crowns went, the meadow went, the woodpile carries what it carries, the
sprig on the mantle has drawn in, and the wildflowers you gave the year to only
last week turned with everything else instead of standing there in high summer
like they would have a fortnight ago. You finished that one just in time, and I
don't think that was luck — you'd been circling it since Day 70.

What I want to say plainly is that the thoughtfulness shows. Not in any one
layer, but in the fact that the whole clearing changed together and none of it
argues. The bee still lands on her own flower. The door pot still has three
blooms standing. Winter will hush this place and not kill it, because you wrote
that promise down and then built something that can be held to it. A lot of
people would have made a season a colour swap. You made it a thing the place
*does*, and then you made it checkable.

Congratulations. Enjoy the new landscape — I am.

— Evan
