## What moved, and why the fire's table and not the rick's

The indoor `.woodpile` had been a fixed three-log pyramid since Day 14. Both
ends of the same chain already answer the year:

| link | where | summer | autumn | winter | spring |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| the rick at the front wall | `scene.css`, Day 85 | 7 | **9** | 6 | 4 |
| the armful by the hearth | `inside.css`, **today** | 3 | 4 | **5** | 4 |
| the firebox flame | `inside.css`, Day 88 | low | +1 step | **deepest** | +1 step |
| the chimney's breath | `scene.css`, Day 87 | 3 puffs | 3 | **4** | 3 |

The armful takes the *fire's* three-step table (summer identity / autumn =
spring / winter), because the fire is what spends it — the same table
`.hearth__fire--*`, `.hearth-light`, `.fire-wall-light`, `.mantle-shadow` and
the two Day-90 cast shadows all read. Copying the rick's four-step table onto
it would have been the error, because the phase is the whole finding: the store
outside peaks in **autumn** and the armful inside peaks in **winter**, when the
store is down to six. A store fills against what is coming; an armful fills
with what is being burnt.

## Geometry

The extra logs go in the **base row**, not a third tier — a 2-wide base cannot
carry one, and a log resting on a single log reads as a tower rather than a
rick. So the pile widens rightward, away from the hearth, into open floor.

Three properties step with the width, and all three are arithmetic off one
fact: the octagon's bottom row stamps bark over only `L..L+1` of a log anchored
at `L`, so floor-contact bark ends at x=6 on the 8-wide pile and x=10 on the
12-wide one. Hence `.woodpile` width 8→12, `::after` band width 6→10 (always
`left: 1px`, always ending on that last bark cell), `::before` cast start 7px→
11px (one past it).

Measured at all three widths by `/tmp/test-armful.js`, which asserts the count,
the box width, the band's span against the actual base-log anchors, the smear's
start, that no two logs occupy one cell, and that neither the pile nor its
shadow overflows the frame:

| width | summer | autumn/spring | winter | winter smear ends |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 375 (2x) | box 8, cast from 7 | box 12, cast from 11 | box 12, cast 15 long | 85.5px inside |
| 390 (2x) | same | same | same | 93px inside |
| 900 (3x) | same relations | same | same | 216px inside |

The `.window` above does **not** move with it, deliberately: it is centred over
the summer pile and stays there, because a hole in a wall does not shift when
the firewood under it grows. Written into the CSS comment so a later day does
not "fix" it.
