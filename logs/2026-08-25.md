## What today changed

`scripts/screenshot.js` (the standing Article-I carve-out) gains **aimed motion clips**, closing the Day-108 question.

- A `MOTION_CLIPS` entry may now carry `seek: { animation, at: [{ frac, label }] }` instead of being sampled on the wall clock. `seekScene()` finds the animation by `a.animationName` off `document.getAnimations()`, reads the round length from `effect.getComputedTiming().duration`, then pauses every animation and sets `currentTime` to the same **absolute** ms on those with `iterations === Infinity`. Absolute rather than per-animation-fraction on purpose: infinite iterations wrap themselves, so every layer lands at its true phase of that second and their relative rates stay honest. Negative `animation-delay` is preserved (it is inside `currentTime`), which is why the blooms still breathe between the two hold frames.
- A clip may also carry `crop: { around, margin, scale }`. `measureCropWindow()` unions the client rects of the named selectors relative to the `.scene` box; it is run **once per seek phase and the results unioned**, so a phase added later cannot fall outside the window. The window is cut from each frame and redrawn at an integer `scale` with `imageSmoothingEnabled = false`.
- `compositeFilmstrip()` gained a fifth `crop` argument; `crop = null` is the pre-existing whole-frame 1:1 path and its output is unchanged (day/night strips still 2140×338, 116/140 KB).
- New clip `bee`: six moments of the 30s `bee-forage` round (0 / 13 / 18 / 36 / 50 / 60 %), cropped to `.flower` + `.sprite--bee` with margin 18 → 59×62 native at ×3.
- Cost: a seek clip does no waiting, so the motion step's wall clock is unchanged. A commit now produces 12 view + 17 state + 4 motion = **33 PNGs**. `pages.yml` and `scripts/views.json` needed no change.
