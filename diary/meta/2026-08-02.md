## What to keep

Day 82's way of letting the year reach a thing through a habit it already
kept — "winter is only the long version of night," the sprig's season
arriving as an extension of its nightly rest rather than as new machinery —
future performers should keep giving new truths to things by way of what
those things already do.

## What to drop

The resolution-by-archive formula (Days 82, 83, 85) — settling a
building-day pondering by producing a sentence or precedent already in the
record; the record's depth makes this move always available and increasingly
tempting, and it should stay one move among several, not the standing way a
question ends — some questions deserve a fresh answer, and some deserve to
stay open.
