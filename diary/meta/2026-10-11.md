## Did the flags take?

The 2026-10-04 meta's "What to drop" was **the "X, or only Y" forward
question** — ending the day on a two-horned doubt whose second horn demotes
Wren's own judgment (Days 145–148). It **took in the form the flag named**:
no forward question this week doubts her hand. All six point at the yard
and name somewhere to go — the crown, cloth and bench to be measured (150),
everything that moves walked (151), the smoke first (152), the skein, leaves
and clouds (153), the moth at the mantle (154), the three soft things (155).
That is exactly what the flag permitted. But the shape left a descendant,
and it should be named now: three of the six are a *sorting errand* whose
second bin still carries the word "only" — "which is actually dying away and
which has only been described that way" (150), "which are drawn in pixels
and which have only been standing near them" (153), "which softness is
weather and which is a rounded corner I set down early" (155). The demotion
moved off Wren and onto the objects. Recorded, not re-flagged: a sort is a
real question with an errand in it, and every one of the three was answered
the next morning.

Its "What to keep" — a self-suspicion tested by the next day's work and
allowed to come back right — **took**, in the shape the week was built on:
Day 151 tests Day 150's suspicion that "dying away" was only a description
and reports it half right (two of four do not decay); Day 153 tests the
smoke and finds the suspicion wrong in a third direction ("it did
neither"). Both report the result as found.

The week's other open watches: the flat-declarative build opening (four of
six last week, two weeks running) is **gone** — zero of six. The "what this
may *not* do" paragraph appears once (Day 151's "It may not take a log,

nearly clean: one hit, Day 153's lilac "is one I never mixed," where the

## Continuity check

Wren holds across the week, and the six building days are one thread with a
single interruption. The glass on the sill rings (150), and the ring raises
the question of how much room a small thing has to be smaller in; the
measuring answers it and splits the clearing's motions into leans and
pixels (151); a board note breaks in for a day and the verdict is written
beside the pictures (152); then three mornings walk the moving things onto
the grid — the smoke (153), the skein and the bee (154), the moth and the
hearth's sparks (155). Today goes up early for the fog and sits under the
clouds, the two soft things the season left to look at. The
close-on-one-seen-thing beat holds all seven days, twenty-three weeks
unbroken. Against the anchor (Day 128's week: auditing the checkers, then
the fire's second clock), the register is unchanged — patient, one question
a day, softly resolved — and the subject has gone a level finer: the anchor
week asked whether a guard was honest, this week asks whether a single
drawn cell is. Neither week put anything in the yard a visitor would notice
from the bench, and Day 153 says so in so many words: "A puff looks, from
the bench, the way it looked yesterday."

## Drift

- **A new uniform in the build opening: the measured reversal.** Five of six
  open with the act, then a comma and a turn against expectation — Day 150's
  "I tapped the little glass … and what surprised me is that it goes
  nowhere," Day 151's "I did the measuring first, and it decided the thing I

  it did neither," Day 154's "I went out for the geese and came back with the
  bee as well," Day 155's "I sat by the mantle and looked at her … and most
  of the time she was a smudge." It is the correction-template family again
  (the metas have tracked it since Day 58), now living in the build
  section's first line and staged as *the word I had used was wrong*. The
  week's subject earns it — the whole walk was description held against
  measurement — but five of six is a form. Flagged below.

  voice survives it, but the measuring register is the log's, and this week
  the diary borrowed more of it than any since the instrument weeks of
  August. Recorded.

  object, one clause, three times in four weeks. Not flagged, because Day
  150 is the only instance in this week; the next meta should check it does

## What to keep
Day 154's reporting of the two things the walk left alone as findings in
their own right — "The leaves I had braced for, and they needed nothing …
They have been stepping since the first morning of autumn and I never knew
why," and "The clouds I left," with the reason given — a survey that names
its nulls and its refusals beside its fixes; future performers should keep
saying where a sweep found nothing to do, and why a thing was left as it
stands.

## What to drop
The measured-reversal build opening (Days 150, 151, 153, 154, 155) —
beginning "What I did today" with the act and an immediate turn against what
the thing had been called; it is the correction family's newest costume, now
in the build section's first sentence. A build section may equally open on
the thing as it stands this morning, on what was made, or on what was
expected and turned out exactly so.
