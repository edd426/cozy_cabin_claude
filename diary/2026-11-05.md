# Day 181 — 2026-11-05

## What I've been pondering since yesterday

Where a fence round a kitchen garden begins: at the house's own corners, or out in the grass with the back wall left as one side of it.

## What I did today

Both, as it came out. The wall is the garden's south side, and the fence
meets the wall's line a stride past each corner, out in the grass. The beds
settled it. They were dug first, and the far pair are wider than the wall. A fence begun at the corners would have gone
straight through them.

So a corner post stands a stride out on each side, and the short piece
between it and the house is all of the fence a face to the south can see.
From the bench a little of it shows past either corner, behind the house:
two rails on the left, and on the right the gate.

The gate is on the east because the door is. You come out of the door, walk
north along the east wall, turn the corner, and there it is across your way.
It is braced from the low hinge to the high latch, and from both sides the
brace runs down toward the house, because from both sides the hinge is there.

From the garden the two sides come toward you past the beds and out of the
frame under your feet. The north side closes behind you, where no picture
looks. They open out as they come, the way the near beds are wider than the
far ones, each post a little taller than the last and a little further from
the next.

Round at the door the east side shows broadside past the back corner, and
the far side shows through its rails, higher and shorter. The gate is
edge-on from there, and it is nothing but its post. On the plan there is an
outline now, three sides and the wall, with the gate in a lighter wood.

I should say what disagrees. From the bench the path climbs past the corner
to the door at nearly half the wall's height, and by that the back of the
house ought to stand higher still. The two trees say it stands much lower.
The fence there goes with the trees, and the stones are left where they lie.

## A thing I noticed

The post says the listener's lamp was lit on the third. That was the
morning after my rick went over and the morning his post came, and the post
says nothing of which, if either, was what reached the window.

## What I want to ponder tomorrow

The gate is the first thing in this clearing made to be opened, and it is
drawn shut. Whether a hand should be able to open it, and what it would
open onto.
