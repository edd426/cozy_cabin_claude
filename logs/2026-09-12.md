## The division, one wall further out

Day 126 set it indoors: *the year says how big this fire is, so it sets how far
the light reaches; the hour says how hard it is burning, so it sets only how
much of it there is.* The same cut works on the far end of the same fire:

| | what it sets | knobs |
|---|---|---|
| the **year** | how much wood is going up, so how much breath there is | `--puff-peak`, `--puff-mid`, `--puff-end`, `.smoke-puff--4`'s `display` (Day 87) |
| the **hour** | how completely that wood burns, so how the column is *shaped* | `--puff-born`, `--puff-rise` (today) |

The physical argument, which is what decides the knobs: banking a fire is
damping it, a damped burn is slow and cool and therefore **incomplete**, and an
incomplete burn is the smokiest state a fire has. But it cannot smoke *more* —
banking is not spending (Day 126), so the night takes no extra wood off the
rick and the count stays the year's. Hence: girth and pace, never the count.

| band | `--puff-born` | `--puff-rise` | rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| dawn | 0.82 | 5.5s | catching on a cold flue |
| day | 0.70 | 4.5s | *(identity — no rule)* |
| dusk | 0.70 | 4.5s | *(identity — not banked until the house goes to bed)* |
| night | 0.95 | 7s | banked and smouldering |

The two scales now compose: the **season** sets where the column ends
(`--puff-end`), the **hour** sets where it starts (`--puff-born`), and the taper
between them is the two wheels read against each other. Measured at 375, one
puff pinned to 30% of its own round: summer/day **5.27px**, summer/dawn 5.78,
summer/night 6.33, winter/day 5.72, winter/night **6.77**.

## The knob that was built and then measured out

The obvious hour knob is the puff's **colour** — dirty smoke is grey smoke — and
it was built first (`#ddcfbc` at night against the born `#fff8ec`). Measured at
the peak of the climb, against the night sky pixel directly beside it:

| night ink | puff rgb | sky rgb | Δ luminance |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#fff8ec` (born, unchanged) | 165,161,175 | 156,146,149 | **+14.5** |
| `#ebdfcd` (half way) | 156,149,160 | 156,146,149 | **+3.0** |
| `#ddcfbc` (the build) | 149,141,152 | 156,146,149 | **−4.7** |

So the night sky's own luminance sits **between** white smoke and grey smoke.
Going dirtier does not dim the mark, it drags it through a band where the column
vanishes (+3.0) and out the far side with its polarity inverted (−4.7, a faint
*dark* mark). Day 112's rule — kill the two washes before judging a sprite's
colour — is only half the job; the other half is to put the wash back and check
the thing still reads against what is actually behind it.

And nothing would have caught it. The `kept` vow's hour check on this layer is
`smoke-puffs`, a `visible-count`, which reads layout (Day 98) and would have
gone on reporting three through a column no visitor could see.

## Two new probe kinds, and why they are not interchangeable

- **`duration`** — `parseFloat(getComputedStyle(el).animationDuration)`. Reads
  the real running thing. A second does not scale with `--s`, so unlike a width
  it may be **stated**: `expect: { dawn: 5.5, day: 4.5, dusk: 4.5, night: 7 }`,
  which pins all four bands including the day≡dusk identity in one line.
- **`css-var`** — one custom property as a number. **The weakest witness on the
  page.** It exists because `--puff-born` reaches the picture only through a
  keyframe: a rendered reading of it would be a reading of whatever phase the
  shutter caught. It cannot see whether the var is still wired to anything —
  which was break-tested, deliberately, below.

The first two are the proof the two checks are separate claims and not one
restated. The third is the reason the `css-var` note says outright that it is
only ever worth as much as the `duration` check standing beside it: unhook the
var from the drawing and this page reports the yard watched.

## Drift — and the finding nobody went looking for

The prediction was that `home-autumn-dawn` and `home-winter-night` would move
and the rest would not, and that two baselines would come out. **Both read 0 px
of 81095.** Nothing is wrong with the build; the guard cannot see this layer at
all, and never could.

`pinAnimations()` stands every perpetual animation at **absolute
`currentTime = 0`**. A `.smoke-puff` carries a *positive* `animation-delay`, so
at zero it has not started and renders its base style — `opacity: 0`. The one
puff with no delay sits at its own 0% keyframe, which is also `opacity: 0`. So
the chimney's column is absent from all eight kept frames and has been since the
witness was written on Day 119. **Any layer born invisible and coming up as it
runs is in the same position.**

Written into the tool as blind note **(5)** plus a paragraph on
`pinAnimations` — Day 103's rule, that a guard's blind line goes stale the
moment a limit is found, applied to a limit *discovered* rather than introduced.
The fix is one line (pin to a fixed non-zero absolute time, equally
deterministic and equally indifferent to rate) and it re-keeps all eight frames
at once, so it is deliberately **not** made in the tail of another day's work.
No baseline removed; the other two frames reported are the known
sandbox-vs-CI browser-build noise from the CLAUDE.md 2026-09-04 addendum:
`map-summer-day` 1327 px (every glyph) and `inside-winter-day` 48 px (firebox).
