Two things worth recording for the next session that touches the runner:

1. **`sprite-tone` had to be given its own page.** Both Node-side pixel
   witnesses work by wrecking the page — `frame-balance` hides every sprite and
   lays a flat grey ground, `sprite-tone` turns the two washes off — so sharing
   one page means whichever runs second reads the other's damage. The failure
   mode is silent in the dangerous direction: with the washes already off,
   `frame-balance`'s wash-on shot has no wash in it, its difference is zero, and
   it reports a perfectly even light in every state forever. `readState` now
   opens a fresh page per distinct `selector|of`; the two probes over the same
   sprite share one pair of shots.
2. **The pixel-diff threshold was bimodal at 6.** Same state, fresh page loads,
   the reading flipping between exactly 67.414 and 67.720 — one half-covered
   edge pixel sitting on the boundary and falling in or out with how the layout
   rounded. Raised to 24 and the spread went to 0.000 across five runs. Not a
   fudge: a pixel the sprite half covers is half grass.
