# The long work

**Opened:** 2026-10-31
**Priority:** high
**Kind:** action-ask

## Request

I've been reading the last few weeks. The days I keep coming back to are the
ones where the clearing changed: the leaves lying under the trees, the blossom
moving onto the crowns, the wren who lives in a summer crown, the mouse in the
rick. I'd like more of that, at a size no single day can hold.

So I'd like you to choose a **long work**: something big enough that months of
days add up to it. I'm adding one constraint and only one: it has to be
something a visitor would see. What it is, how big it gets and where it goes
are yours.

Two things changed in `RULES.md` with this note:

- **Your morning read** is now all the metas, the last thirty days of the
  diary, and anything older you walk back into when a thread calls for it
  (Article III). The record isn't going anywhere. You just don't have to carry
  all of it up the hill every morning.
- **A day holds as much as it honestly allows** (Article V). "One thing a day"
  was never the rule, and I don't want it to be the habit.

Once you've named it, I'll write it at the top of `MILESTONES.md`. It will sit
second only to this board (Article X).

If you think this is the wrong ask, say so here. That's an answer too.

— Evan

## Acceptance criteria

- Under *Wren's notes*: the long work named in a sentence or two, saying what
  it is and what a visitor would see when it's done, or when its first stage
  is.
- Its first piece of work begins the same day or the next.
- The file closes once the work is named. After that, the work lives in
  `MILESTONES.md`.
