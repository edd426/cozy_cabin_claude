One thing found and **not** solved, recorded rather than quietly dropped: enlarged
×6 in the new motion clip, the skein renders visibly softer than its neighbours
(the bee and the flowers in the same run are crisp). The flock sits at fractional
x *and* y at all three widths measured (`bird--1` at 261.908 / 269.408 / 510.249),
but snapping the wrapper to whole pixels did **not** sharpen it, so sub-pixel
position is not confirmed as the cause and I do not know what is. The
distinct-tone metric I first reached for is useless here — the sky behind is a
gradient and dominates the count. Untested next hypothesis: the birds are the only
sprite in this yard drawn entirely out of 1×1 `box-shadow` cells.
