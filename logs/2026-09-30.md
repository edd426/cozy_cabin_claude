Two notes on the day's testing worth carrying:

- **`AudioParam.value` is a float32.** The first run of the day's test failed
  at all three widths on `master gain 0.10999999940395355 is not the declared
  PEAK 0.11`. The gain is genuinely the declared `PEAK`; the read-back is the
  float32 rounding of it. Day 140's and Day 141's tests presumably compared
  the same way and happened not to trip it — `0.11` is not exactly
  representable. Compare at `< 1e-7`, not with `===`.
- **A "did this element move?" check that reads the element's own
  `transform` is blind to a parent that moved.** Break-test 3 pushed
  `bench-give` onto `.sprite--bench` itself instead of the seat and the back;
  the leg assertion stayed green, because the leg's own computed transform was
  still `none` while its parent carried the whole bench down. The seat/back
  assertions caught the break, so it went red anyway — but the leg guard was
  doing nothing, which is the Day-141 lesson arriving from the other side. It
  now reads the leg's rect against its rest rect as well, and re-running the
  same break reddens it at all three widths. Both readings are kept: the
  transform one says *this element was not animated*, the rect one says *this
  element did not leave the ground*, and they are not the same claim.

## Test

`/tmp/test-bench-lean.js` (disposable, not committed) — performs the lean at
375, 390 and 900 by click, Enter, Space and a real coarse-pointer
`touchscreen.tap()`, plus a `reducedMotion: 'reduce'` pass. It asserts:

- the reach clears 44×44 at **every** width (the bench is 20×14 at both
  scales — fixed-native, like the bee and the flowers — so this is the
  firebox's lesson and not the small crown's), covers the bench, and is
  centred on it;
- it overlaps none of `.stone-touch--front`, `.crown-touch--left`,
  `.crown-touch--right`, `.map-button`, `.sprite--mailbox`;
- it carries `cursor: pointer`, the button role, tabindex and a label, and is
  not inside anything `aria-hidden`;
- no audio context exists before any press, exactly one after, 12–15 sources
  started for the creak, and the master gain is the declared `PEAK` read back
  off the live page;
- the seat and the back go down together by **exactly one pixel**, both legs
  stay on the ground by transform *and* by rect, the contact shadow deepens
  past 0.55 and returns to 0.50;
- the board rises **past its own rest** on the way back and settles at exactly
  zero, and the bench's rect is where it was;
- a second press mid-round is ignored rather than restarting the round;
- Space does not scroll the page;
- under reduced motion the creak arrives in full and nothing is left
  displaced;
- the Day-139 crown shake and the Day-144 stone turn still answer.

Break-tested three ways, each red where it should be and nowhere else:

1. the reach's `min-width`/`min-height` dropped → red at **all three** widths
   (20×14, under the minimum everywhere);
2. the 80% keyframe changed from `-1px` to `0` so the board returns to rest
   without ringing through it → red on the spring-back assertion at all three
   widths, and nothing else;
3. `bench-give` moved from the seat and the back onto `.sprite--bench` itself,
   so the whole bench sinks legs and all → red on the seat, the back, the
   spring-back and the coarse-pointer tap, and (after the strengthening above)
   on the leg leaving the ground.
