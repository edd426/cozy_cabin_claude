## Environment notes

Nothing broken. Plain `git push` worked. Chromium came from the pre-staged
`/opt/pw-browsers/chromium_headless_shell-1194`. Four things worth recording:

1. **The sandbox's hour is a test variable.** The routine fires ~00:10 UTC and
   `sky.js` bins that as `night`, which is exactly the band where the porch lamp
   is lit anyway — so the first pass of `/tmp/test-lamp.js` could not tell a
   working `data-lamp-init` guard from an absent one, and I removed the guard as
   decoration before finding out. Pinning the clock to a noon instant with the
   Day-95 `freezeClock` shim (`page.addInitScript` + `timezoneId: 'UTC'` on the
   context) made the fade plainly measurable and put the guard back. **Any
   in-session test of an hour- or season-gated layer should pin the clock.**
2. **Two of four break-tests passed on the first round**, i.e. two assertions
   were decoration. One (the storage claim) was because the test seeded
   `localStorage` by hand before the return visit and so never checked that a
   *press* writes anything; one was (1) above. Both rewritten; both now redden.
3. **`check-drift` locally reported 3 frames.** `around-autumn-day` (90 px, box
   23×48 at the near tree's foot) is today's leaf-size fix — baseline removed and
   the removal committed beside the change per the Day-119 addendum, and CI
   re-kept it. `map-summer-day` (1327 px, every glyph) and `inside-winter-day`
   (64 px, the firebox) were byte-identical to their documented figures and are
   the known sandbox-vs-CI browser-build noise; left alone.
4. Standing checkers all green locally against `http://localhost:8099/`:
   `check-almanac` **82 claims / 56 probes**, `check-gallery` (home/around/inside,
   16 states each), `check-nesting` (13 relations, all declared). None of them
   changed by a line: a held state is invisible to every one of them, because
