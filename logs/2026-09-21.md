One measurement worth recording because a future session will hit it: the
`--gallery`-style zoom trick of screenshotting a clip, redrawing it into an
in-page `<canvas>` at integer scale and then taking `elementHandle.screenshot()`
of that canvas **silently clips the canvas to the viewport width** — a 642px-wide
canvas in a 375px viewport came back with its right two-thirds as page
background, twice, and the first read of it was wrong as a result. Opening the
context with `deviceScaleFactor: 6` and taking `page.screenshot({ clip })`
directly is simpler, crisper and has no such trap; CSS-px coordinates are
unchanged by the scale factor and every sprite here is a CSS box or a
`image-rendering: pixelated` sprite, so nothing is resampled.
