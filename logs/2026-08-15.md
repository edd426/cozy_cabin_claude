## What today added

Day 98 gave the almanac's *countable* sentences witnesses. Day 98's closing
question was whether that left a guard around the easy half — the three things
this place actually promises (winter is a hush not a death; nothing here is
ever lost; the light never gets an address) had none.

- **New third check kind in `window.CabinAlmanac.CHECKS`: `floor: N` + `over: [states…]`.** The
  reading must never fall below `N` in *any* state in `over`, and `over` is
  always the whole round (4 seasons or 4 bands) rather than the states a
  sentence names. A description wants a reading at a named state; a refusal
  wants a floor asked everywhere.
- **`VOWS` published** on `window.CabinAlmanac` alongside PROBES/CHECKS. A check
  carrying `vow: '<key>'` renders under its vow in the new `#almanac-vows`
  block rather than in `#almanac-checks`.
- **10 vow checks, 2 new probes** (`tree-crowns` visible-count, `tree-crown-fade`
  opacity). The "given back" half of *nothing is lost* needed no new machinery —
  it is `rising` on the wrap-around pair (`['night','dawn']` for the candle,
  `['spring','summer','autumn']` for the woodpile).
- **The third vow carries no witness and says so on the page**, in its own
  prose, where a witness would have stood. Evenness is what a measurement is
  worst at; a list that read as complete while covering two of three would be
  the failure the almanac exists to refuse.
- `tools/check-almanac.js`: `stateNames()` gained a `check.over` branch,
  `verdictsFor()` a `check.floor !== undefined` branch ahead of `expect`.
  Nothing else changed — the runner still keeps no list of its own.

Totals: 20 checks (was 10), 12 probes (was 10), 10 states, 24 view-reads (was
19). The three new states are `spring|day` on *inside* and `summer|dawn` /
`summer|dusk` on *home*. `pages.yml` needed no change; the check still runs as
the screenshot job's last step, after the previews are pushed.
