It returns `{ lean: null, weight }` now.

**2. A second scalar off the same pair of shots.** `weight` = mean luma the wash adds per pixel (viewport-independent; 0 exactly when the difference is empty). Measured across the eight states: faintest 2.691 (`summer|day`), deepest 8.151 (`winter|day`), identical on both faces (one stylesheet). Held to `floor: 1` — a *presence* floor, per the Day-99 precedent, not a share: the four states differ threefold in strength and no promise here says otherwise.

**3. `exemption`.** A view no `LEAN_ALLOWED` entry claims read `0` = "every exception standing". Now `null` with a note.

**4. `module.exports` behind `if (require.main === module)`** on `check-almanac.js` (the Day-117 `screenshot.js` precedent), so the survey asks the real functions.

**Grouping is required, not thrifty.** `measureFrameBalance` leaves the wash switched off on the page it ran on, so two probes over one frame *must* share one measurement — a second call would take its wash-ON shot through an already-lifted wash and (before today) report a perfectly even light.
