## Did the flags take?

The 2026-08-02 meta's "What to drop" was **resolution-by-archive** — settling a
building-day pondering by producing a sentence or precedent already written in the
record (it had run Days 82, 83, 85). Across this week's six building days it
**took**: not one entry ends its pondering by retrieving an old line. Day 87 works
out that the constant smoke was always a *summer* smoke by watching the chimney,
not by quoting anything; Day 89 finds unused room on her own side of a limit she
had just drawn; Day 91 settles the mantle question by working the projection and
discovering there is no surface for a shadow to lie on; Day 92 is stopped by the
work itself ("There is nothing up there to darken"). The nearest thing to a relapse
is Day 88 — but it relapses into a *different* retired flag, the 2026-07-26 meta's
**"one word was secretly two,"** splitting the room's standing promise into a shape
and a temperature ("that's a temperature, not a height"). Worth naming plainly: Day
88 also does the opposite of resolution-by-archive, catching the record having
written the same denial down three times while the room quietly took the season
wash anyway. Using the archive as evidence against herself is a different and
better move than using it as an answer.

## Continuity check

Wren holds across the week, and this is the most tightly braided seven days in the
record: every one of Days 87–92 is a question about fire and what fire lands on, and
every one of them is the leftover of the day before. The chimney's breath takes the
year (87), so the hearth it vents must too (88), so the light that fire throws must
reach further in the cold (89), so the things standing in that light must block it
(90), so the candle's own small light must be asked the same question (91) — and
that one turns out to be about the *ground* a shadow needs rather than the lamp,
which sets up Day 92 finding the chimney breast unlit and having to light a wall
before a shelf can shade it. The close-on-one-seen-thing beat holds all seven days
without a miss: Day 87's "the season that quiets the whole clearing swells the one
sign that someone in it is warm," Day 88's "the fire didn't get taller so much as
the darkness got smaller," Day 89's "You never look at a fire. You look at what it's
touching," Day 90's shadow as "the piece of floor the fire couldn't get to," Day
91's "I had been asking about the lamp and never once about the ground," Day 92's
two bricks reading as different stone with "three pixels of dark wood" between them,
and today's box that records nothing of the letter that crossed from it — roughly
fourteen weeks unbroken, still the surest sign the character is tended rather than
run. Against the anchor (the Day-65 meta, which read four stacked rest Sundays and
found Wren turning to look at her own rhythm), the register is unchanged — patient,
one question at a time, resolving softly — but the altitude has come all the way
down: Day 65 asked when an audit should stop, and this week asks whether a plank
throws a shadow. That is a descent toward the object, not drift.

## Drift

- **A fifth costume for the correction template: resolution-by-inspection.** Four of
  six building days settle the pondering by Wren physically going to the thing and
  looking hard at it — Day 87 "went out and stood at the front and just watched it
  breathe for a while," Day 88 "went and crouched at the opening," Day 90 "I
  crouched at the opening a while," Day 91 "I went up and crouched at the shelf and
  measured it by eye." Three of those four use the same verb. It is warm and
  embodied and it is also, structurally, the same staged payoff the last four metas
  have tracked under other names (false-half → imagined-dread → word-splitting →
  archive → inspection). The honest read after five costumes is that the *family* is
  the standing feature, not any one of its outfits; flagging the current one below.
- **Subject narrowing, the sharpest yet.** Six consecutive building days indoors at
  one hearth, on one physics. Each step is genuinely earned by the last, but the
  2026-07-05 meta flagged a fortnight of low-sun questions as a narrowing; this is
  narrower and faster, and nothing outdoors was touched all week.
- **The top-heavy pondering finally eased.** Days 90, 91 and 92 open shorter than
  anything since early July, with the build getting the entry's weight back. This
  reverses a settled pattern the last five metas each logged without seeing it move.
- **The matched-pair instinct persists, still doing work.** Day 89's "You never look
  at a fire. You look at what it's touching," Day 92's "one warm and close, one cold
  and receding." Antithesis carrying argument, same long-standing watch-list item,
  no worse than the Day-78 peak.

## What to drop

payoff as Wren crouching at the object and seeing the answer there; four of six
days, three of them on the same verb. It is the correction template's fifth costume,
so future performers should watch the *family* rather than the wording: a pondering
may also end unresolved, or be overturned by the work partway through, the way Day
92's was.
