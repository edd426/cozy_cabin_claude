## What has no witness, said plainly

The claim that makes this face's fall *its own* — that each leaf finishes in the column it let go from, where a front leaf finishes further right — is held by nothing on the almanac. Every check kind there returns a count, an opacity, a width or a colour statistic, and **a count cannot see a path**. That limitation is written into the new check's own `guards` string rather than left for someone to discover, and the only place the claim is *visible* is the new `-motion-leaves-door` strip beside `-motion-leaves`. A strip is evidence, not a guard.

## Measurements that decided the build

`.sprite--tree-near` is native-px-sized inside a `%`-sized frame, so it covers a different fraction of each layout. Crown box, as a fraction of the scene:

| width | `--s` | crown x | crown y | crown foot | trunk foot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 375 | 2 | 77.4–94.9% | 52.0% | 84.7% | 94.0% |
| 390 | 2 | 78.2–95.0% | 53.9% | 85.2% | 94.1% |
| 900 | 3 | 82.4–95.5% | 63.7% | 88.1% | 95.1% |

Every leaf start sits inside the overlap of all three (x 83–94, y 74–80) so a leaf is always in the canopy when it lets go; every settle at 95.5–96.5%, which is grass at every width. Leaf size is `calc(3px * var(--s)) × calc(2px * var(--s))` rather than fixed px, because the tree beside it is drawn native-px × `--s` and a fixed-px leaf would sit off the crown's own grid at one of the two layouts.
