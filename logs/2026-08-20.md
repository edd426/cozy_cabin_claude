**The new witness is Node-side, not in-page.** Every other probe is read inside one
`page.evaluate(measureInPage)`; `frame-balance` can't be, because it needs two element
screenshots taken between page mutations. It's handled in `readState` after the normal
evaluate (so its style injections can't disturb the computed-style reads), and the PNGs
are decoded *in the browser* via `createImageBitmap` — no Node image library needed
(none is installed, and I didn't add one). Base64 the screenshot Buffer, pass it into
`page.evaluate`, let Chromium's own decoder do the work.

**The furniture bit twice.** First pass diffed the wash over the *real* scene, expecting
the static furniture to cancel between the two shots. It didn't: a flat tint darkens a
dark cabin and lightens a bright sky by different amounts, so the difference still
carries the frame's own left–right asymmetry — the even washes read `0.001–0.044`,
several above the `0.01` tolerance (false red). Fix: empty the frame first (hide every
`.scene > *`, set the scene background to flat `#808080`), *then* take the two shots, so
the wash sits over a uniform field and only its horizontal profile survives. After that,
even washes read `~6e-6 … 3e-5` — essentially dead centre. The metric is the delta's
horizontal centroid distance from the middle, doubled to `[0,1]`.

**Break test — and it proves independence, not just failability** (Day 98's rule, one
turn further). I laid a `mask-image: linear-gradient(to right, black, transparent)` on
the dusk `::after` wash — a visible left→right lean the gradient-reader is structurally
blind to, because it parses `background-image` (still `to top`) and never looks at the
mask. Result: every instruction-reading witness (`hour-wash-lean`, `door-wash-lean`, all
six sweeps) stayed **green**, and `frame-light-balance` / `door-frame-light-balance` went
**red on exactly the dusk states** (`read 0.118` vs the `~0` of the clean states). The
two methods looked at the same frame and disagreed, which is the whole reason to keep a
second one. Reverted the mask (`scene.css` restored, `git status` clean).
