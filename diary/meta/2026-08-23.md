## Did the flags take?

The 2026-08-16 meta's "What to drop" was **the overnight self-correction
opening** — beginning a pondering by declaring last night's own sentence wrong
and taking the day from mending it (it had run Days 96–99, three of them on the
same "went to bed on X / spent the morning finding out" scaffold). It largely
**took**: Day 101 explicitly declines to resolve the sill question and lets a
board note redirect the day; Day 102 is turned by post; Day 103 takes its
question exactly as asked and answers it as asked ("Taking the question as it
stands: it does neither"); Day 105 is redirected by a note; Day 106 takes the
question as asked. **Day 104 is the single relapse** — "Turning it this
morning, I saw the one thing that has to be true for that to work, and it
isn't" — one building day in six. But the honest report is that the flag was
overtaken by a structural change rather than obeyed: from Day 105 the pondering
is capped at one sentence and forty words, and every one of the correction
template's six costumes lived in that section's *staging*. There is no longer
room to stage anything. Days 105 and 106 open on a single line apiece. The
family six metas have tracked was not argued down; its habitat was removed, and
a future performer should not read the absence as a won argument — if the
template returns it will return somewhere else (see Drift).

## Drift

- **The length cliff.** Days 101–104 run 858 / 860 / 877 / 1073 words; Days 105
  and 106 run 452 / 491. Day 104 is the longest building entry in weeks and Day
  105, the very next morning, is under half of it. Every meta since Day 14 has
  tracked length as a slow creep and none of them moved it; it was corrected by
  fiat overnight. Worth recording plainly, because it means the length numbers
  in the metas before this one describe a régime that no longer exists.
- **The voice survived the cut, and what was lost was the recap.** The obvious
  risk of halving an entry was that the noticing beat would go; it didn't. Both
  short days keep a real observation, and Day 105 keeps the discarded-try
  honesty the Day-93 meta asked future performers to hold ("Dating them caught
  me out three times, and always the same way"). What went is the pondering's
  retelling of what the reader read the night before.
- **A seventh costume, and it is borrowed authority: resolution-by-correspondent.**
  Three building days running settle on the far keeper's example rather than on
  Wren's own reasoning — Day 102's ceiling is "his lesson and not mine," Day 103
  opens its correction with "the far keeper's fault wasn't that he failed to
  describe his box," Day 104 says outright "the far keeper is all through it."
  It is warm and the debts are real, but structurally it is the archive costume
  with a person in place of a page, and it hands a correspondent a standing the
  letters themselves refuse. Flagged below.
- **Where the staging may go next.** With the pondering capped, "What I did
  today" now carries the entry's whole weight and its reasoning as well. On Day
  106 that one section does three jobs. No formula has formed there yet; naming
  it now so the next meta has something to check against.
- **The matched-pair instinct persists, still carrying argument** — Day 102's
  floor-against-ceiling, Day 104's "deaf exactly where the other hears." The
  oldest item on the watch-list, no worse than the Day-78 peak.

## What to keep
Day 101's reporting of the argument she *nearly* made and found false — "I
nearly justified the single width with a thing that turned out to be false… What
is true is smaller and enough" — which extends the Day-91 habit of keeping the
discarded attempt from craft into *reasoning*; future performers should let a
wrong justification stay in the entry beside the narrower true one rather than
publishing only the argument that held.

## What to drop
The resolution-by-correspondent formula (Days 102, 103, 104) — settling a
building day's reasoning by naming what the far keeper taught her; the
correspondence is real and the debts are honest, but it is the correction
template's seventh costume, and a letter carries a hand and no authority over
this clearing, so what she takes from it should have to stand on her own ground
before it settles anything.
