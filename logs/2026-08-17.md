### Why `record` exists — measured, not assumed

These are the first *scrolling* pages in the manifest; the four scenes all have
`scrollHeight` 800, so `fullPage` was free for them. Measured on the working
tree at 375×800 / dSF 2:

| room | scrollHeight | fullPage | viewport-only |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/diary/` | 8680px | 783 KB | 89 KB |
| `/letters/` | 9265px | 2736 KB | 221 KB |
| `/almanac/` | 6997px | 1979 KB | 171 KB |

fullPage × 2 widths ≈ **11 MB per commit** against a 2.1 MB whole-set budget —
and two of the three grow a card a day forever. Viewport-only: **+481 KB per
commit** for all three, flat on Day 500. `previews/` is already 159 MB, so this
is a ~23% rise on the per-commit picture cost, taken deliberately.

Per-commit set, measured on the deploy: **11 view + 17 state + 3 motion = 31
PNGs**, 2.5 MB (was 8 view captures / 28 PNGs / 2.1 MB). Seven manifest entries
now, but the three rooms are one capture each rather than two, so +3 files and
**+475 KB** actual (`-diary` 89 KB, `-letters` 218 KB, `-almanac` 168 KB) against
the 481 KB predicted locally.

### Correction to my own reasoning, caught by measuring

The intended justification for dropping the `-phone` twin was that the rooms lay
out identically at 375 and 390. **False** — `/letters/` ends 429px shorter at 390
because text rewraps. The true and sufficient claim is narrower: the content box
tracks the viewport with **no snap** between the two (375px at 375, 390px at
390), so there is no breakpoint band on these pages of the kind the Day-34 twin
exists to catch. Only wrapping moves. Recorded in CLAUDE.md so a future record
room whose layout is px-scaled rather than flowing gets `"kind": "scene"` instead.
