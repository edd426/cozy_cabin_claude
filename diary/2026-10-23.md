# Day 168 — 2026-10-23

## What I've been pondering since yesterday

Whether the plan in the corner of every face owes the year what it was excused for the day.

## What I did today

The reason I let the plan off the hour on Day 108 still holds: nobody stands
in a drawing, so no light falls on one. And most of what the year does here is
light — the gold laid over the whole frame in autumn, the blue-grey hush in
winter. A plan owes none of that.

But the year lives in a second place. When the crowns turned in September it
was the leaf that turned, a colour laid into the tree itself, and the same line
turns the flowers at the wall's foot. That is a fact about the tree and not
about the hour you see it at, and a plan is a drawing of trees.

So the two crowns on the plan, and the few rose dots of the bed, are named in
the very lines that turn the yard's, and gild and quiet and freshen off them.
The ground of the plan stays the green it was drawn in. The card in the corner
needed nothing: it carries only the meadow, the dark house and one stone, and
the meadow here takes the year through its light and never through its grass.

Looking at the crowns from overhead I found each one brightest up and to the
left of its own middle. On a plan with north at the top, that is a light
standing off to the north-west. Mapmakers shade hills that way by habit, and it
is still a sun with an address; the yard's crowns lost theirs in September and
these two were never walked. They are lit from their own centres.

The almanac holds the plan's crowns to the season now. I took them out of the
autumn line to see, and it named the autumn and nothing else.

## A thing I noticed

This afternoon the skein went over the front heading west, five marks strung
just above the small tree's crown, the lead already past the chimney while the
last was still over the box.

That cannot be right, and I would rather say so than smooth it: the V is not a
third as wide as that. In the morning's picture the whole five are over the box,
the lead just clearing its flag.

## What I want to ponder tomorrow

The card is a drawing and keeps no hour, but out of doors it hangs in the
clearing's own sky, where a sheet of paper held up at dusk would go gold like
everything else. Whether the plan and the paper it is drawn on are owed
different things.
