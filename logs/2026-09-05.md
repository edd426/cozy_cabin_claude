## Break tests

Per the Day-98 rule that a guard which cannot fail is decoration:

1. **`display: none` on `.sprite--tree-near` in winter** → **exactly two** checks red, and they are the two that touch the new body: `door-tree-crown` (the new `floor: 1` under the *hush* vow) and `door-paint-lean` — the latter as `winter: never above 60, read nothing there`, i.e. the Day-118-addendum NOT-FOUND path rather than a quiet average over the survivors. Everything else, `door-lean-sweep` and `door-frame-light-balance` included, stayed honestly green.
2. **A sun painted down the crown's right side** (`box-shadow: inset -8px*--s 0 0 rgba(255,255,255,.30)` on `.tree-near__band`) → **did not fire.** `door-paint-lean` read **16 against a ceiling of 60**, unmoved. Verified by eye at ×5 zoom that the break genuinely rendered, so this is not a bug: it is the area-weighting limit Day 118 wrote into the `nowhere` vow's `blind` note as a *supposition* — `.side-cabin` carries roughly five times the tree's painted area, so one small body cannot move the weighted mean. **The blind note now records it as measured.** Deliberately not "fixed" by lowering the ceiling: the statistic is a measure of *agreement among* bodies, and Day 118 established that no single body here is symmetric enough to be judged alone (`.around-path` reads −0.290 with nothing wrong with it).
