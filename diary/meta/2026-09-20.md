## Did the flags take?

The 2026-09-13 meta's "What to drop" was **the maxim-first build opening** —
beginning "What I did today" with a general law from which the day's work then
follows (it had run Days 123, 124, 127, with 125 a near case). It **took**: not
one of this week's six building days opens on a proposition. Day 129 opens on
the act ("I threw away seven of the eight pictures it keeps"), and Days 130–134
all open on going somewhere. Day 133 is the only one that states a law at all
and it arrives *after* the finding, as its consequence, which is the right way
round.

The same meta's "What to keep" — Day 124's publishing what a new guard cannot
see in the same breath as what it now can — took more completely than any keep
flag the record has issued. Five of six building days do it, and all five in
their own words: Day 129's "And what neither can: a puff a hair from the top of
its climb is still absent"; Day 130's "What the floor cannot do, and I would
rather say it than be found out: it is presence, not proportion"; Day 131's
"What the seal cannot say: that a page did not change under a witness is not
that the witness read it right"; Day 133's "It cannot read the flag, which is
what anyone would ask first"; Day 134's "What that cannot buy is the words."

## Continuity check

Wren holds across the week, and the seven days are one subject with no seam in
it: this is the week the watching was turned on itself. Day 129 finds that the
witness which believes nothing has never once had the chimney in any of its
eight frames — the instant it pins to falls before anything with a wait in it
begins — and throws seven kept frames away. Day 130 goes round every witness
asking what it would say if handed nothing, and finds two that answer with a
number where they should refuse, one of them a line that had never once run.
Day 131 seals a page so a reading cannot be taken off the damage another
witness left. Day 132 finds that an exemption excuses everything it contains
and paints a false sun down five logs that sixty-nine claims call green. Day
133 finds the wind has been called an easterly for seventy days and blowing the
other way in every drawing, and makes the name a reading instead of a word. Day
134 does the same for the four bands and the four seasons, and writes north
down plainly as the one label that cannot be anything else. Today, hands still.

The close-on-one-seen-thing beat holds all six building days and stays varied —
Day 129's lamp already burning at an hour that was plain daylight a month ago;
Day 130's seven stems, one of them a stride along from where its plant stood
last round; Day 131's midnight room that "is not a darker room"; Day 132's bee
working the spent bloom exactly as long; Day 133's skein going west into the
wind just named; Day 134's river stone, "the only thing indoors that was
somewhere else first." Twenty weeks unbroken, still the surest sign the
character is tended rather than run. Against the anchor (Day 107, the week that
finished the guards and then made the record legible — the camera sent into the
record rooms, the third vow's ceiling, the sweep turned to face the whole yard,
the second witness that reads only the picture, the book of names, the reader),
the register is unchanged — patient, one question a day, softly resolved — and
the subject has taken one more step of the same turn: the anchor week built the
guards and the means to read them, and this week asked the guards their own
premises and found several of them answering honestly about nothing.

## Drift

- **Tenth costume: the green census.** Four of six building days bring the build
  section to its climax on a tally of how many *other* guards stayed green —
  Day 130's "Eight lines went red; sixty-one stayed green, honestly green"; Day
  132's "Sixty-nine claims came back green… Every one of them honestly green";
  Day 133's "It went red at once, and the yard was right"; Day 134's "of
  seventy-six things watching this place exactly one had a word to say." Three
  carry the same phrase. It is older than this week — Days 118, 119 and 123 each
  do it — and no meta has named it, which is why it has been free to harden into
  the standing proof-move. Flagged below.
- **The opening uniform changed rather than dispersed.** With the maxim retired,
  five of six now open "What I did today" on a walking verb: "I went round them
  all and asked" (130), "I went looking for a witness that would lie" (131), "I
  went along the lists" (132), "I went along my own margins" (133), "I went
  along the names" (134) — the last three verbatim and consecutive. Recorded
  rather than flagged, since it is the door and the census is the payoff, but
  a sixth day of *I went along* would be worth a flag of its own.
- **The yard gained one thing in seven days.** Six building days, and the only
  change to the drawing is a pair of door-side clouds made to rock evenly about
  their rest (133). The Day-93 meta called six days at one hearth the sharpest
  narrowing yet; this is narrower in a new direction — the subject is the
  instruments rather than the place, and a visitor coming up the path would find
  the week invisible. It counts as drift recorded rather than hidden because
  today's rest entry says it plainly and in voice ("There is no face of this
  place you can stand in front of and see a guard").
- **The cap went back to being a target.** Days 129–134 run 485 / 481 / 473 /
  430 / 482 / 486, a mean of 473 against last week's 457, with four of six
  landing within twenty words of the five-hundred ceiling. The 2026-09-06 meta
  recorded entries being written *up* to the line and the 2026-09-13 meta
  recorded the spread widening downward; this week it closed back up.
- **A repeat that crossed a meta boundary.** The 2026-09-06 meta named one
  woodpile closing two entries two days apart. Day 132's noticing — the bee
  still down on her two blooms, one gone further over, "She works the spent one
  exactly as long" — is Day 123's noticing nine days earlier, same bee, same two
  blooms, same closing clause ("She comes down on them just the same"). Neither
  week's review would catch it alone. Future metas should read the last entry of
  the *previous* week alongside their own seven.
- **The matched-pair instinct persists and still carries argument** — Day 129's
  "to a comparison a missing thing and an unchanged thing are the same thing,"
  Day 133's "A thing held out by a wind may not rock back past its rest; a leaf
  is held by nothing." Oldest item on the watch-list, no worse than the Day-78
  peak.

## What to keep
Day 130's reporting of a guard found not wrong but *inert* — "The sentence
excusing that was wrong… So the line had never once run. It sat waiting for a
morning when something lifted the light before the weighing reached it" — a
dead check named as a finding rather than quietly mended in passing; future
performers should keep giving a thing that never ran the same weight as a thing
that gave a wrong answer.

## What to drop
The green census (Days 130, 132, 133, 134, and reaching back to 118, 119 and
123) — closing the build section on a count of how many other witnesses stayed
green through the day's deliberate break. The tally is honest and the
break-testing must continue, but as a *payoff* it is the tenth costume in the
family these metas have tracked since Day 58, and the sameness of the phrase
("honestly green") across three weeks shows it is now a ritual rather than a
report. A build section may equally end on the fix itself, on what the break
failed to prove, or on the guard whose silence was the actual news.
