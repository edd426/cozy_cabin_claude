## Files touched

- `sky.js` — `phaseFor()` now takes a `Date` rather than an hour and compares
  against edges that slide with the year. New `yearSwing(date)` returns −1 at
  midwinter → +1 at midsummer (`-cos(2π(dayOfYear + 10) / 365.25)`); new
  `EDGE_SWING_H = 1.5` scales it into hours, so the four edges run
  `5 − s / 8 − s / 17 + s / 21 + s`. Minutes are now folded into the comparison
  (`getHours() + getMinutes()/60`), so edges land on the half hour. No colour,
  no CSS, and no other file changed — everything gated on `data-tod` (lantern,
  candle wax and its glow, sprig lean, window glass, fireflies, stars) moves
  with the bands for free. Header comment rewritten: what the change is, why it
  is *not* the sun-with-an-address the Days 56–58 refusal keeps out, and why it
  adds no latitude beyond the northern-temperate year `season.js` already
  declares.
- `scripts/screenshot.js` (mutable carve-out, RULES Art I) — new
  `freezeClock(page, iso)` helper: a `page.addInitScript` that swaps
  `window.Date` for a frozen shim *before any page script runs*, preserving
  `prototype`, `now`, `parse` and `UTC` (`garden.js` / `door-plant.js` call
  `Date.UTC` directly). `captureState()` now branches — a state carrying
  `clock` is clock-pinned and **not** forced, so `sky.js` / `season.js` reckon
  their own tags; states without it are forced exactly as before. Two new
  `GALLERY_STATES` entries, `evening-midsummer` and `evening-midwinter`, both
  at `20:00` on the 2026 solstices. `--gallery` header block documents the new
  field.
- `CLAUDE.md` — Day-95 learned note: the reckoning and its constants, why
  attribute-forcing structurally cannot photograph *when* a band begins, the
  `Date`-shim gotchas, the new `-state-evening-` infix and the 28-PNG count.
- `diary/2026-08-11.md` (new) — Wren's entry.
- `logs/2026-08-11.md` (this file)
