## The finding (the day's argument)

The lean sweep excuses by `el.closest(a.selector)`, so a `LEAN_ALLOWED` entry
reaches its **entire subtree**, not the element its written reason names.
`.scene--inside .woodpile` is on that list for the cast shadow the armful
throws — and the armful's five `.woodpile__log` children live inside it.

Break: `background-image: linear-gradient(to right, rgba(255,255,255,.35),
rgba(0,0,0,0))` on `.woodpile__log`.

```
check-almanac: 69 claim(s) witnessed by 46 probe(s)
HELD  room-lean-sweep — nothing in the room leaning sideways at any hour ...
HELD  room-lean-sweep — the same of the room in all four seasons
HELD  room-exemptions — the chair's shadow and the woodpile's still running away ...
HELD  room-exemptions — the same in all four seasons ...
HELD  home-paint-lean — ...
HELD  door-paint-lean — ...
check-almanac: OK — every witnessed claim still holds, and every reading was
taken on a page nothing else had worked on.
```

All 69 green. The sweep skipped the logs (`closest` excused all five —
verified directly), and the exemption entry's only `holds` is `opposed`, which
sums lean **signs** over the subtree, so a log's lean is absorbed into the
shadow's own sign rather than named. The room also has no body-weighing at all:
`paint-lean` is asked only of the two outdoor faces.

**Gotcha, and the first run of this experiment was vacuous because of it:**
`.woodpile__log` sets `background: #d4a373` as a *shorthand*. A
`background-image` inserted above it is reset to `none`, so the break drew
nothing and the green meant nothing. Declaration re-placed after the shorthand
and the computed `backgroundImage` verified on all five logs before the run was
believed.

The selector cannot be narrowed: exact-match-instead-of-`closest` works for
five of the six entries but not `.sprite--bee`, whose exemption genuinely
covers a child (`.bee__wings` carries its own leaning gradient). So the reach
is declared rather than removed.
