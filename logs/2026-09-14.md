## The fault, measured before it was fixed

`pinAnimations()` stood every perpetual animation at absolute `currentTime = 0`.
`currentTime` **includes the delay**, so zero is before the start of anything
carrying a *positive* `animation-delay`; such an element renders its **base
style**, not a keyframe.

A `/tmp/` probe on the live home view, reading `getComputedTiming()` before and
after the seek:

| element | delay | `progress` @ 0 | `progress` @ 8000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.smoke-puff--1` | 0 ms | `0` | `0.143` |
| `.smoke-puff--2` | 2333 ms | **`null`** | `0.810` |
| `.smoke-puff--3` | 4667 ms | **`null`** | `0.476` |
| `.leaf--1` | 0 ms | `0` | `0.615` |
| `.leaf--2` | −6000 ms | `0.375` | `0.875` |
| `.flower__head` ×5 | 0 / −1000…−5000 | `0`–`0.714` | `0.2`–`0.947` |
| `.sprite--bee` | 0 ms | `0` | `0.267` |

`.smoke-puff`'s base style is `opacity: 0`, and `@keyframes smoke-rise{,-lean}`
is `opacity: 0` at 0% — so puffs 2 and 3 were drawn as base style and puff 1 at
its own invisible 0%. **The chimney column was absent from every kept frame
from Day 119 to Day 128.** Nothing else was affected: every other animated
layer here carries a zero or negative delay, and `@keyframes leaf-fall-*` is
`opacity: 1` at 0%.

The only positive delays in the repo are the three smoke stagger values,
`calc(var(--puff-rise)/3)`, `/3*2` and `/2` (Day 127). At the longest rise —
the night's 7s — the largest is **4667 ms**, which is what 8000 clears.

## Why seven frames and not eight

Every frame that holds a moving layer is a picture of the old instant and had
to go. `map-summer-day` did not: a probe on `/map/` returned
`document.getAnimations()` → `[]`. It holds no animation at all, so the new pin
cannot move it — and it reported the **identical 1327 px** before the change,
after the change, and after the re-key, which is the known Day-119
sandbox-vs-CI browser noise (every glyph of every label struck, no shape moved)
and not a finding. Its CI-made baseline stays.

The seven were `git rm`'d and committed as the declaration (Day-119 addendum).
The tool re-keyed them locally during verification; those sandbox-made PNGs
were **deleted before the commit** so CI keeps its own, per the same addendum.
CI's run on `194f7d1` should therefore print seven `KEPT … no picture stood for
this` lines, `HELD map-summer-day`, and exit 0.
