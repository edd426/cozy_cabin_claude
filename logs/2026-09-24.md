## Design notes worth keeping

- **A `<img>` renders no `::before`**, so the Day-81 mailbox invisible-tap-pad
  trick cannot be played on the two front crowns directly. The reach is a
  transparent sibling `<span>` instead — which also means the drawing is not
  touched by one pixel, which is why no drift baseline moved.
- **`min-width`/`min-height: var(--tap-min)` on a `right:`-anchored pad grows it
  leftward.** That is the only reason the small right crown (30px wide at
  `--s: 2`) is tappable on a phone. Break-tested: dropping the widening reddens
  the tap-target assertion at 375 **and** 390 and stays green at 900, where the
  3x sprite is 45px and clears 44 on its own — the Day-34 phone blind spot,
  caught by the test rather than by luck.
- **Computed `display` ignores a `display:none` ancestor.** A leaf inside the
  hidden leaf-fall layer still reads `block`, so "is the leaf showing" must be
  asked of `getClientRects()` or of the class, never of `getComputedStyle().display`.
  My first summer assertion was wrong for exactly this reason.
- **An exponent-aware number regex, for matrix parsing.** A near-rest skew comes
  back as `matrix(1, 0, 1.20637e-06, 1, 0, 0)`, and `/-?[\d.]+/g` splits
  `1.20637e-06` into two tokens and reads the wrong one — my "it ends at rest"
  assertion failed at `1.2064` on a crown that was at rest. Use
  `/-?\d*\.?\d+(?:e[-+]?\d+)?/gi`.
- **Why no witness was added.** Every `CHECKS` entry on the almanac varies on
  `axis: 'season' | 'tod'` (or names dates via `on:`); a tap is gated on neither,
  the same slot the bee's round has never had (Day 108). An honest witness would
  have to *perform* the interaction, which is a different kind of instrument from
  anything standing here. The founder's note said to build the thing first and
  its instrument second, so the gap is recorded rather than filled — in the
  message's notes, in `touch.js`'s own header, and here.
- **What the standing witnesses do see, and it is nothing, on purpose.**
  `crown-shake` exists only while the class is on, so the Day-133 drift probe —
  which pauses every animation and walks each named layer — finds only
  `tree-sway` on a resting page. The shaken leaves are `display:none` at rest, so
  `falling-leaves` still reads 4 and `door-falling-leaves` 3, and `check-gallery`
  skips an element that shows in no state (`if (!showsIn.length) continue`).
