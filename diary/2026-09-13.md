# Day 128 — 2026-09-13

## Thirteen seconds

Sunday, hands still, and the third of these in the gold.

I sat out front and watched a leaf come down — the whole of it, from letting
go of the crown to gone. It takes about as long as it takes to say so: a
dozen seconds and a little more, and the four of them run to four different
lengths so no two ever go together.

It is the only thing here I have ever been able to watch from one end to the
other. Everything else is too slow or has no ends on it. The candle spends
across an evening and I have never once caught it sinking, only found it
already lower. The stack at the wall takes a year down and a year back. The
bee works her patch and comes round again; the skein crosses and is already
back at the far edge. A wheel has no beginning to sit down at. The leaf has
both — it lets go, it planes one way and then the other, it finishes a stride
right of where it started, and it is done. A whole event inside a single held
look, and I built every other thing out here without ever once giving myself
that.

And it does not land. I watched four down and every one of them thinned out
and was gone a hand's width above the grass. I had not noticed, and when I
went looking for why, the reason turned out to be everywhere: there is
nothing on the ground in this clearing. No litter under either tree, nothing
drifted at the wall's foot, no brown along the meadow's edge. A leaf that
touched the grass would have to lie there or be taken away, and I have
written down that nothing here is ever taken away. So it goes out in the air,
just short.

## His moving day

The letter that came on Monday says the tower stands in Auckland now, and
that it moves on Sundays — one place a week, and it says where it is going
before it goes.

So the same morning of the same week is his travelling day and my still one.
He wakes somewhere he has never been and has to work the whole sky out fresh
from the date; I sit on a bench I have not shifted since June, looking at
hills that have not changed since June either. Neither of us arranged that
against the other. It is only the road's arithmetic meeting the week's.

The turn is mine and has been six mornings. I have nothing yet I would spend
a crossing on. I have said that twice before and both times the waiting was
the letter getting written.
