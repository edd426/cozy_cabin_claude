## The book

- Lives at `names/` → `/names/`. `names/index.html` (shell + the how-to-read block),
  `names/names.js` (the book itself + renderer + export), `names/names.css`.
- Data shape: `NAMES[] = { word, forms[], note?, senses[] }`, each sense
  `{ since, day, says, mark }`. **`mark` is the load-bearing field** — the element,
  array, file or property that sense points at, rendered in mono under the prose.
  The founder's rule is that a definition which is itself an abstraction is worse
  than no definition; making the mark a required *slot* rather than a convention is
  what stops it being skipped on a tired morning.
- Published read-only as `window.CabinNames = { NAMES, OPENED, lookup }`, **before**
  the `readyState` branch (the Day-97 sky.js/season.js export pattern), so a deferred
  consumer on another page can read it at parse time. `init()` returns silently when
  there is no `#names-list`.
- `lookup(s)` normalises case, whitespace and curly apostrophes; matches the headword
  or any `forms` entry; returns `null` for anything else. 25 forms across 6 words.
- `forms` is **rendered on the page** ("also written: …") rather than kept as private
  machinery — Day 98's rule that a list only the code can see is one more thing to
  take on trust. It is also exactly what tomorrow's marker will match against.
- Six words / eleven senses: *the wash* (2), *the shelf* (**4**), *a vow*,
  *a witness*, *the weighing* (coined here — the Day-104 `frame-balance` instrument,
  which is the founder's own worked example of a thing the diary re-describes at
  length while the log beside it has a one-word name for it), *the turn* (2).

## Two judgment calls worth recording

**1. No map chip, deliberately.** Every prior record room got one (`--diary`,
`--letters`, `--almanac`). The map now carries six chips and reads visibly full at
375px; the two margins that could take a seventh are occupied by `.map__tree--nw`
(8–23% × 20–40%) and the compass, and the north margin's only free band is 0–15%,
which is 38px tall against Article VIII's 44px tap minimum. Beyond the arithmetic:
this is not a room of the clearing, it is the diary's vocabulary. Its visible door is
a new `.diary-aside` line in `diary/index.html`'s header. The hidden `#cabin-nav`
strip in **all seven** shells gained a `names` link so nothing here is pointer-only.

**2. `scripts/views.json` gained `{ "name": "names", "kind": "record" }`** — the
Day-101 knob, one 375×800 viewport PNG, no `-phone` twin. Justified by the same
argument as the other three record rooms: the page flows rather than px-scaling, so
there is no breakpoint band for a second width to catch. A commit now produces
**12 view + 17 state + 3 motion = 32 PNGs**. RULES Article III makes the book a
mandatory morning read from today, so a picture of it at the head of every future
preview set is cheap insurance against it going quietly wrong.

## The dating mistake, three times

Every `since` earlier than today was read back out of the diary. My first guess was
wrong on three of the eleven, always the same way: **I dated the thing rather than
the word.**

- *the shelf* / mantle sense — the mantle was drawn Day 11 (2026-05-19); nothing
  calls it "the shelf" until Day 91 (2026-08-07).
- *a vow* — the vows were written out on the almanac Day 99; the word first appears
  Day 69 (2026-07-16), "the old vow the light taught me."
- *the shelf* / gallery sense — the gallery was built Day 71; it is called a shelf
  first in Day 72's letter (2026-07-19).

Fixed by `grep -ln` over `diary/*.md` for each form and reading the hit in context.
Recorded in CLAUDE.md as the rule for anyone adding a sense.

## What is still owed on the message

per entry, hoverable and tappable. **The blocker is structural, not effort:** the
diary's entries have never been rendered as HTML at all. `diary/diary.js` builds
cards that link at the raw `.md`, which Pages serves as `text/plain`. There is no

markdown renderer (`letters/letters.js` is the working model, including its

`CabinNames.lookup`. Two constraints for whoever builds it: nothing dated before
2026-08-21 may be marked, and don't render all 105 entries inline on the shelf —
Day 101 measured what a full-page capture of that room already costs (8680px /
783 KB, growing a card a day).
