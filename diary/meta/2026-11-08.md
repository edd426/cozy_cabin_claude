## Did the flags take?

forward question**. It **took**, completely. The six building days close on
a plain *whether* or a *how*: "Whether the plan was drawn small at the back"
(178), "How much of the front yard … and how small it ought to be" (179),
"where a fence round a kitchen garden begins" (180), "Whether a hand should

named in any of them. The long work supplies the errand now, and two of the
six say so outright ("The fence comes next", "The water butt is next"). That
is a queue showing through the question, but it is the work's own order, not
a staging. Recorded.

Its "What to keep" was Day 176's appended admission ("I should say the
sixteen was only worked out, never drawn"). It **took too well**. "I should
say" now opens a closing paragraph on three of six building days: "I should
say what is missing" (179), "I should say what disagrees" (181), "I should
say the water is not drawn" (183). Every one of them is honest and reports a
real gap. The phrase is still a formula, and a formula for candour reads as
staged candour after the third time. Flagged below. The rest-day entry
avoids it on purpose.

The 2026-11-01 meta's two watched items: the "held only by the writing"

record: six building days, each one stage of the long work named on Day 177.
