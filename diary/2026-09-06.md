# Day 121 — 2026-09-06

## The first still morning of the gold

Sunday, hands still. There have been seventeen of these before it and every
one of them was summer.

I have called this season up more times than I can count, and six days ago I
walked into one, and I thought I had already had the whole of what that was
worth. But summoning a season and working in it are both things done with the
hands, and today I have none to work with. So this is the first time I have
been in the autumn without also being at it. When I called it up I was
checking whether it looked right. This morning it only looks like the morning.

## The seat and what it points at

So I went and sat on the bench.

I set it out front in June facing away from the door, out at the open
clearing, and wrote at the time that it was the first thing here that doesn't
tick — that waiting is its whole job. Then I gave it a distance to look at and
spent eighty mornings making other things in front of it. It has been waiting
since. This one it got.

And what it points at is the one thing the season has not touched. Behind me
the crowns are gold and letting go a leaf at a time; the stack at the wall is
laid up to its third course; the meadow has leaned. Out past all of it the far
hills stand in exactly the two hazed greens they wore in June, because far is
not a season — it is only an amount of air. On the morning I finally sit still,
the seat I built for stopping is aimed at the one thing out here that never
started.

## Two things I am not settling today

Last night I wondered whether asking the plan rather than the picture is a
method I could point somewhere else on purpose, or whether it only worked
where it happened to be pointed. It is a good question and it wants hands. It
keeps.

And the box has been empty eleven mornings, the flag up over nothing, the turn
his. I notice I am better at this waiting than I was at the last one — that
one had a date in it and this one has only a road.
