  Day 162 has "both faces went red, and nothing else did". This week has
  Day 164's "the count named the winter and nothing else", Day 165's "the
  count named the day and nothing else", Day 166's "The count named the
  summer and nothing else", Day 167's "it named the spring and nothing else"

  would notice"). The census counted the survivors; this counts the single

  reports a reading instead ("Out front at midnight there is plenty"), and
